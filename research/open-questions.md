# Open questions

1. **Battery.** Can the module truly run 7 days? Bench test the sensing duty cycle, BLE interval and charging losses.
2. **Heater power.** Single-use cell in the patch: size, cost, disposal, and connector design.
3. **Skin-temperature safety.** The thermistor must sit at the skin, not at the module base. Independent hardware cutoff needed.
4. **Signal validity.** Does abdominal surface EMG/EHG track cramp severity? Needs a pilot with a clinical partner.
5. **Adaptive Comfort Engine.** Does tap-based personalisation improve outcomes over a manual button?
6. **Price.** Module price (EUR 39-49) is a proposal. Test willingness to pay.
7. **Regulation and privacy.** Menstrual data is special-category under GDPR. Wellness framing needs legal review.
8. **Ideas from outside drafts worth evaluating:** TI ADS1292R AFE, Nordic nRF52840, IMU for motion rejection, electrode contact-impedance check, BLE GATT profile, 4-screen app structure. Claims from those drafts that were rejected: 14-18 h battery with a direct heater rail, "Annex XVI fast-track", HIPAA applicability, "validated clinical summary" wording.
